QA records preserve question, answer and option fields; their JSON layout includes row/source/license metadata. This projection does not license underlying RGB, scans, camera tracks, object banks or reference definitions. Computed geometry/features/tensor caches are excluded from distribution and generated by the user. Original reference/object/training annotation remains necessary where specified and keeps its source-specific access terms. Maintainers holding the original native records can migrate them with [PREPARATION.md](PREPARATION.md) without replacing historical targets.

The VSTI-Bench dataset card does not state a data license. This package therefore does not redistribute VSTI questions, answers or videos; it includes only member IDs. Download the benchmark from the provider and follow the provider's terms.

The VLM-3R/VSTI camera-label correction dated 2026-07-13 changes labels and membership. The project QA and evaluation members identify the versions used by the saved runs; current provider downloads are distinct.
